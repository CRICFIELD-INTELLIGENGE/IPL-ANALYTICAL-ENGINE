# Scouting service

A deterministic analytics layer on top of `global.db`. Every figure is a calculation over stored counts; nothing is generated by a model or an LLM. The service is a library plus command-line tools in `scripts/src/scout/`. It is not mounted in the API server or used by any page; that needs separate approval.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `pnpm --filter @workspace/scripts run scout:build` | Rebuilds the derived tables in `global.db`: per-innings usage, season roles, per-ball variance, season coverage and player attributes. It also runs a self-check that the league adjustment is unbiased. Takes about 12 s. |
| `pnpm --filter @workspace/scripts run scout:demo` | Runs the three demonstration queries and writes `lib/cricket-data/global/reports/scout-demos-<date>.{md,json}`. Pass `-- --player <cricsheet id>` to profile a different player. |
| `pnpm --filter @workspace/scripts run test:global` | Runs the global-engine and scouting tests, 19 in total. |

Run `scout:build` after every `global:sync` that changes data.

## Derived roles (`roles.ts`, rules version `roles-v1`)

Roles describe how a player was used, not how well they did. They are season-specific and stored per player, competition and season in `scout_roles`. Every row stores the inputs that produced it.

The inputs come from every ball, with super overs excluded:

- **Arrival order:** the order in which each batter first appeared at the crease. Positions 1 and 2 opened.
- **Entry point:** the number of legal balls already bowled when the batter arrived.
- **Phases faced and bowled:** the share of a player's balls in the powerplay, middle overs and death.
- **New ball:** whether the bowler bowled the first or second over of the innings.

**Batting role.** Rules are applied in this order; the first that matches wins.

1. Fewer than 3 innings → `insufficient`
2. Opened in at least 50% of innings → `opener`
3. Median position 8 or lower → `lower_order`
4. Median arrival after 72 legal balls (over 13 onwards), or ≥50% of balls faced at the death with median position ≥5 → `finisher`
5. Median arrival before 36 legal balls (during the powerplay) → `top_order`
6. Otherwise → `middle_order`

**Bowling role.**

1. Never bowled → `none`. Fewer than 60 legal balls → `insufficient`.
2. Fewer than 1.5 overs per innings → `part_time`.
3. Exactly one phase reaches 1.6 times its natural share of the bowler's legal balls → `{phase}_specialist`. In T20 the natural shares are 6, 9 and 5 of 20 overs, so the thresholds are ≥48% powerplay, ≥72% middle and ≥40% death.
4. Otherwise → `multi_phase`.

The thresholds scale with phase length. A flat 50% threshold would make death specialists almost impossible, since the death phase is only 5 of 20 overs.

**Usage profile:** `batter`, `bowler`, `all_rounder`, `keeper_batter` (stumpings plus a batting role) or `limited`.

**Window roles.** The same rules applied to any range of seasons. They are computed on demand from `scout_batting_innings` and `scout_bowling_innings`.

## Ratings (`service.ts` → `ratePlayers`)

**Query parameters**

- discipline (batting or bowling)
- competitions
- phase (`all`, `powerplay`, `middle` or `death`)
- seasons window: either the last N seasons or explicit seasons per competition
- `minBalls` (default 120)
- optional role filter
- optional player keys

**League adjustment**

- **Expected count.** For each player-season, expected = the player's balls × the league rate for the same competition, season and phase. League totals are sums over every player, so summed over all players expected equals actual exactly. `scout:build` checks this on every competition.
- **Index.** 100 × actual ÷ expected. 100 is the league average for the balls the player actually faced or bowled. This corrects for era and for league scoring differences, but it is not an equivalence between leagues.

**Metrics**

- **Batting:** strike rate, boundary %, dot % (lower is better) and balls per dismissal. The balls-per-dismissal index adds 1 to both expected and actual dismissals, so a player with no dismissals still gets a finite value.
- **Bowling:** economy (lower is better), wicket rate (balls per wicket is displayed; the index is on wickets per ball), dot % and boundary % conceded (lower is better).

**Uncertainty**

- **Shrinkage.** shrunk = (balls × index + 120 × 100) ÷ (balls + 120). Small samples are pulled toward the league average, so a 190 strike rate over 35 balls ranks below a 155 strike rate over 600 balls (tested).
- **95% intervals.**
  - Strike rate and economy use a normal approximation with that league-phase's per-ball variance (`scout_phase_variance`). A player's own variance can't be observed from aggregates, so these intervals are approximate.
  - Proportions (boundary %, dot %, wicket rate, dismissal rate) use a Wilson score interval.
- **Percentiles.** Mid-rank percentile of the shrunk index within the population, direction-aware, so 100 is best.
  - The population is the same competition, same phase and same window, with at least `minBalls` balls, plus any role filter.
  - Rows below `minBalls` get no percentile.
- **Sample tiers.** `rankable` at 120 balls or more, `low` at 60–119, `insufficient` below 60.

**Coverage indicators**

- **Partial seasons.** A season is marked `partial` when it has fewer than 60% of the competition's median season match count. Rating rows list any partial seasons inside their window.
- **Lower-division share.** The share of a player's balls in matches the source labels "Plate Group" (SMAT 2020/21 and 2021/22). It is reported, not adjusted for.

## Other functions

| Function | Returns |
|---|---|
| `resolvePlayer(ref)` | A player by Cricsheet id or exact written/register name. An ambiguous name returns every candidate and no player. |
| `playerAttributes(key)` | Bowling type, batting hand and 2026 capped status, each with source and confidence, or `unknown`. |
| `seasonLines(key)` | Every competition and season: team, coverage, season roles, batting and bowling lines, and league-relative indices. |
| `trends(key)` | Per competition, the latest season against the player's earlier seasons on the league-adjusted index. A trend is `improving` or `declining` only when the 95% interval of the difference excludes zero, otherwise `stable`. It is `insufficient sample` below 60 balls on either side. |
| `similarPlayers(key, …)` | Nearest players on standardized shrunk indices plus phase usage, within the same discipline and among rankable samples only. When the target's bowling type is verified, only bowlers of the same verified type are compared. |
| `strengthsAndWeaknesses(rating)` | Metrics at or above the 75th percentile (strengths) and at or below the 25th (weaknesses), worded from the numbers. |
| `iplAppearances(key)`, `debutSeason(key, code)` | Facts from the match squads. |

## Attributes Cricsheet does not provide (`enrichment.ts`)

Only named sources and recorded links are used. Rows that cannot be linked to exactly one player go to `attribute_link_issues` and are never guessed.

| Source | Gives | Link rule | Result (2026-10-10) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `artifacts/api-server/data/player-bio.json` | Bowling style, batting hand | `playerName` equals the IPL match-file name of exactly one player. Bio names used by several entries are ambiguous and not linked. | 771 of 788 linked exactly; 12 with no candidate; 5 ambiguous |
| `2026 ipl auction player list*.csv` | Official Capped/Uncapped/Associate, bowling style, hand, country, specialism | Exact full-name match, plus corroboration: the state association matches a SMAT team (`manifests/state-associations.json`) or the 2025 IPL team matches. Confidence is `corroborated_unreviewed`. | 101 of 369 linked; 201 no candidate in the loaded matches; 67 not corroborated; 0 ambiguous |

**Bowling type** comes from keywords in the written style:

- **spin:** off-break, leg-break, googly, orthodox, wrist-spin and similar
- **pace:** fast, medium, seam
- **mixed:** both kinds listed
- **unknown:** neither

When both sources give a type, an exact link wins over a corroborated one, and disagreement is reported as `conflict`. **Unknown is never treated as spin.**

**Two link methods were tried and rejected:**

- **Initials-form auction names.** "M Kumar" linked Manvanth Kumar to Mukesh Kumar, both in Delhi's 2025 squad.
- **Bio entries sharing a name.** Two "R Bishnoi" entries carried different bowling styles.

## Known limitations

- No ball-tracking, line, length or speed outside the IPL-only commentary in `sqlite.db`.
- Bowling style exists only for players covered by the two sources above. Most BBL, SA20, CPL and ILT20 bowlers, and many SMAT bowlers, are `unknown`.
- Opposition strength is not adjusted. SMAT Plate Group exposure is shown as a share of balls only.
- Approximate intervals for strike rate and economy (see above).
- **League adjustment is within a league, not across leagues.** No league-strength multiplier exists until one is validated against players who moved between leagues. That validation is part of the franchise-fit design (Step 5, not approved).
