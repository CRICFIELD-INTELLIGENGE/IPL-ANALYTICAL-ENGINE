# Retention Intelligence Engine V2

Delivery report, 2026-11-04. Snapshot `cf-intel-2026.78d0eb4c09`, data to 2026-05-30, rating `crr-2.0`. Nothing is committed or deployed. The full numbers are in [`v2-report.json`](v2-report.json), which `node --import tsx scripts/retention/v2-report.ts` regenerates.

## What changed and why

The V1 index (`rri-1.0`) already used several seasons, so a single bad season was not the main fault. It had four structural flaws:

- **Money counted twice.** Economics and rule efficiency together carried 20 points.
- **The price model under-prices elite retained players.** Few of them ever reach an auction, so there are few sales to learn from.
- **Scarcity was a second performance rank.**
- **Fit measured only the marginal gain.** Squad depth zeroed out starters.

V2 fixes these in the method. No player's score was adjusted by hand.

| Component | Weight | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| Performance | 30 | Three seasons weighted by balls faced and bowled, with empirical-Bayes shrinkage. Phase-, venue- and opposition-adjusted. A season with no matches adds nothing. Percentile among squad players of the same role. |
| Role scarcity | 25 | Gap to the best unsigned player in the same granular role and nationality (70%), and teammates who already cover the role (30%). |
| Auction economics | 20 | Expected price against the contract, as a percentile. Left out when the salary is missing or the price model is outside its training range. |
| Tactical fit | 15 | The optimiser's change to the best XII (50%) and whether the player is in it (50%). No captaincy bonus. |
| Age and cycle | 10 | The measured age effect over the next three seasons. Left out where the birth date is unverified. |

**How the rating is assembled.** Each component is a percentile. Weights are rescaled over the components that could be measured, and coverage records how much weight was left out.

**Insufficient Evidence** applies below 60% coverage, or when a player has no record in the last three seasons.

**Granular roles.** Examples: Indian death-overs pacer, overseas new-ball pacer, wrist spinner, seam all-rounder, left-handed middle order, wicketkeeper-batter, opener.

**Death and new-ball labels** need 120 balls bowled and come from the player's phase split.

**Low confidence** means less than 75% coverage or fewer than 10 matches in three seasons. Such a player cannot reach the top two tiers and is held at Strong Retain, with the reason stated. Seven players are held there.

**The forecast is separate and unchanged.** It still fails its calibration gate (error 0.13 against a 0.08 limit), so it publishes no percentages. CRR is never converted into one.

## Validation

**Performance weighting, out of time.** The table shows rank correlation with next-season output.

| Weighting | 2016–23 (choice) | 2024–26 (reported) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.50 / 0.35 / 0.15 (brief's starting point) | 0.162 | 0.195 |
| 0.60 / 0.30 / 0.10 | 0.163 | 0.195 |
| Equal three seasons (chosen) | 0.164 | 0.182 |
| V1 halving decay | 0.161 | 0.198 |
| Latest season only | 0.141 | 0.180 |

- The three-season weightings are statistically indistinguishable. Each beats a single season.
- The pre-declared rule picked "equal" by 0.002, and that result was kept.
- On 2024–26 the brief's 0.50/0.35/0.15 does slightly better, but changing the choice after seeing the holdout would be tuning to it.

**Against real retention decisions.** This is a ranking test (AUC) on 850 out-of-time decisions from 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2026.

| Ranking | AUC |
|---|---|
| Latest season only | 0.682 |
| 0.60 / 0.30 / 0.10 | 0.672 |
| V1 decay | 0.666 |
| 0.50 / 0.35 / 0.15 | 0.664 |
| Equal three seasons | 0.637 |
| Forecast model (all inputs) | 0.782 |

Franchises lean on the latest season more than next-season output justifies. CRR follows the output evidence, so it will disagree with some real decisions by design.

**Age curves.** These are measured from IPL history, net of regression to the mean, and are small:

- Batting: −0.5 runs per match at 29–31.
- Bowling: −0.4 at 25–28.

They replace V1's editorial age factor.

**Calibration.** The tier bands are the brief's provisional bands. There are no 2027 decisions yet to calibrate them against. Brier and calibration error apply only to the forecast (0.222 against a 0.231 base rate; ECE 0.13), not to CRR, which is not a probability.

**Sensitivity.** Four other weightings were tested: equal, performance-led, money-led and no-age. Mean tier stability is 0.82. Unstable players are listed in the JSON.

- Statuses: Rumour, Reported Interest, Negotiations Reported, Officially Confirmed, Completed, Denied, Stalled, Superseded.

## Data defects found

- **Four players played for a franchise in 2026 but have no 2026 contract row:** Sai Sudharsan, Sai Kishore and Prasidh Krishna (GT), and Chameera (DC). V1 left them off their squads and counted them as unsigned. They are now placed from the 2026 match record, with salary unknown. GT's best-XII strength rose from 11.54 to 13.82.
- **Moeen Ali is flagged Indian** in the source data.
- **Six squad players have no nationality:** Varun Chakaravarthy, Suryavanshi, Payne, Smaran, Dubey and Dahiya. They are labelled "Nationality unverified" and never assumed Indian.
- **36 squad players have no salary and 118 have no birth date.** Those components are left out, not zeroed.

## UI

The Retention Room keeps its design. The CRICFIELD Picks tab is now the sortable Franchise Retention Board: CRR, the five component percentiles, salary, and the change from V1. Sort chips appear on phones.

Each player's breakdown shows:

- the components with evidence
- three seasons by phase
- a scenario simulator: Retain, Release and buy back, Release and replace, and Trade where a report exists
- confidence and sensitivity
- age and cycle, with the 2028 mega-auction view marked as conditional
- financials, replacements and limitations

New tabs: **Trade Intelligence** (sourced ledger with history) and **Model Transparency** (weights, bands, the validation tables, age curves).

Squad Impact remains the squad-level simulator. At 375px wide no page overflows.

**Synced elsewhere:** player profile card, Scout row, War Room, AI Analyst (CRR, components and scenarios), and the Squad DNA tiers. All of them read the same `pickFor` value.

## Limitations

1. **The weights and tier bands are not calibrated.** Calibration needs the real 2027 decisions, due after 17 Nov.
2. **The unsigned pool is small (36 rated players)** because released players are not known until the deadline. That makes scarcity look higher than it is.
3. **No injury or availability data**, and fielding is not rated.
4. **WAR is not produced.** No validated runs-to-wins conversion exists, so the unit is runs above replacement per match.
5. **The 2028 view assumes a mega auction**, which the BCCI has not confirmed.
6. **The buyback chance comes from the price model's interval, not a bidding model.** It is withheld whenever that model is out of range.
